A Note

A Little Something From Me

I didn't want to give you something that could simply be bought and wrapped. So I made something that exists only for you — a small world made from words, moments, feelings, and one very special surprise.

There isn't a store for the things I actually want to give you. There's no box for how it feels to build a life with someone, one ordinary day folded into the next until they add up to something enormous. So instead, I built this — a quiet corner of the internet that only opens for you, on a day that belongs entirely to you.

You are the person I choose, again, without needing a reason to. Not because of one perfect moment, but because of how it feels to simply keep going — together, unhurried, certain. That's the part I wanted to put into words, even though words were never going to be quite enough.

So before anything else happens on this little page, I just want you to know: this was made slowly, and on purpose, and only for you.

— With everything I have

The Beginning

Some things start so quietly you don't notice them becoming important. A conversation that wasn't supposed to matter. A day like any other. And then, without any warning, it turns into the beginning of everything else that follows.

♡

The Little Moments

It was never the big occasions that stayed with me the most. It was the small, unremarkable ones — talking about nothing in particular, waiting for a reply, laughing at something that wouldn't make sense to anyone else. Those are the moments that quietly built us.

✶

What I Carry With Me

There are certain feelings that don't fade with time, no matter how ordinary the day was that they came from. I carry those with me, on the days we're close and the days we're apart, and they never once feel any less certain.

✨

What Comes Next

I don't need to know every detail of what's ahead to know I want you in all of it. Whatever the future holds, I'd like to keep building it slowly, patiently, and honestly — the same way we've built everything so far.
